Featured demo

Wildfire intelligence: from satellites to answers

After the 2023 Evros wildfire in Greece, the questions were simple: what burned, how badly, and what did it hit? This project answers them in one place, from open satellite and map data: burn severity, impact on buildings, roads and protected nature, checked against the EU's official burnt-area map, and plain-English questions answered on the map.

  • 96,496 ha burned area mapped
  • 0.93 agreement with EU map
  • 60,420 ha in protected areas

Featured demo

Flood intelligence you can talk to

During Pakistan's 2022 floods, district officials had no fast way to see which areas and buildings were underwater. This dashboard maps the flood and the risk zones, and lets anyone ask a question in plain English, such as “which buildings are within 500 m of the flood?”, and see the answer on the map.

  • 670,000+ buildings
  • 12,500+ km² district
  • 100% offline AI
